# Welcome to Lobby

Lobby is Dark Burn's own whiteboard: a place for brainstorms, storyboards, retros, workshops and client reviews, the way you used Miro, built and run by us.

## Getting in

- Open **https://draftroom-staging-web.onrender.com** in Chrome, Edge or Brave (Safari and Firefox work too).
- Sign in with your work email (darkburn.com, trailerparkgroup.com or trailerpark.com). Lobby emails you a 6-digit code, so you don't need a password. Google sign-in works too.
- First time? You'll see a short Welcome with three ways to start: a blank board, a template, or the Lobby tour (a 5-minute hands-on board that shows the basics).

## The basics

- **Sticky notes:** press **N**, click to place, type. Pick colours and sizes from the Styles button.
- **Shapes, text, arrows:** the toolbar at the bottom. Select a shape and just start typing to label it. Arrows stick to the notes they connect.
- **Frames:** group a section of the board (and they become slides when you present).
- **Images and files:** drag them onto the board. PDFs, Office files, Photoshop, Illustrator and TIFF files get page previews. Videos get a poster frame. Right-click a picture and choose **Remove background (beta)** to cut it out.
- **Find anything:** **Ctrl+K** searches the board and runs any command ("make the yellow notes blue" works).
- **Undo** with **Ctrl+Z**. Everything saves as you go.

## Elements: icons, kits and your team's library

- Open **Elements** in the left panel. One search covers all of it.
- **Icons:** over 6,500 icons. Click to add one, or drag it to a spot. Pick its colour and size first.
- **Shapes:** arrows, callouts, status stamps, retro and SWOT kits, device frames and storyboard panels. There are also deliverable frames at exact sizes with safe zones (Steam capsules, YouTube banner, stories and reels, social posts).
- **Library:** select anything you made (a logo, a sticker, a frame layout) and choose **Save to library**. Save it for the whole workspace or just for you, in folders, with tags. Anyone on the team can then drop it onto any board.

## Storyboards

- **Storyboards:** Ctrl+K → "Storyboard" lays out numbered panels with shot notes. You can also paste a script and let Lobby draft the panels. Already drew your frames? Select them and choose **Make storyboard from frames**.

## Working together

- **Share** (top right): invite teammates by name or email, or copy the board link. Clients and freelancers can join with a guest link and just a name, without an account.
- **Live:** see each other's cursors, follow someone's view, comment and @mention, and react to notes with emoji.
- **Run a session:** Facilitate gives you a timer, dot voting, a laser pointer and private brainstorming. Present turns frames into slides.
- **Since your last visit:** come back to a board and Lobby highlights what others added or changed while you were away.
- **Links between boards:** paste a board's link (or Ctrl+K → "Link to another board") to drop a card that opens it. Each board shows which boards link to it.

## Client reviews

- In **Share → Guests and clients**, create a **client review link**. Clients open a clean review page, with no account needed.
- Select a frame or file and choose **Mark for review**: clients can approve it or ask for changes. Rounds keep each version, so you can compare them side by side. Clients can comment on a video at the exact frame.
- A **Review summary** PDF collects every decision and comment for the record.

## AI helpers

- Select notes and use the AI button to group them into themes, summarise them, or turn them into a mind map or diagram.
- **Capture the room:** Ctrl+K → "Capture the room…" turns a photo of a real sticky-note wall, or a meeting transcript, into a board. The first time, Lobby tells you what is sent to OpenAI; click Continue.

## Moving in and keeping copies

- **Import from Miro (beta):** on the dashboard, choose **Import → Import from Miro (beta)…** and follow the steps. Notes, shapes, text, frames and connectors come across. Images arrive as placeholders for now.
- **Backups:** the board menu's **Download backup (.lobby)** saves a whole board as one file. **Import → Lobby backup (.lobby)…** restores it as a new board.
- **Export** a board, a frame or a selection as PNG, SVG or PDF. PDFs keep real, searchable text and stay small.

## Slack alerts

- A workspace admin can connect a Slack channel in the workspace menu's **Chat notifications…**. You then choose which alerts you want: mentions, replies, access requests or review decisions. Nothing is sent until you opt in.

## Help and feedback

- The **Help** menu on the dashboard has the tour and the keyboard shortcuts.
- Found something broken or have an idea? Use **Feedback** (top bar). Every message is read.

## Good to know

- This is our pilot. Things will keep getting better every week, and "What's new" on the dashboard tells you what changed.
- Big boards are fine: thousands of notes stay smooth to pan and zoom.
- On a phone you can open boards, add notes, comment and follow along. The full toolset is best on a computer.
