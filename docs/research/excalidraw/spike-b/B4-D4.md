## What the room does (element-room.ts `drainBatch`, `commitFailed`)

The hook arms `failCommits`; the next drain's COMMIT throws before `COMMIT` runs. The room rolls back,
answers every push of the batch `UNAVAILABLE`, closes and reopens its board file from disk, rebuilds its
caches (index, groups, counts), drops the snapshot cache, and closes every session with 4000 `RECONNECT`.
A `DISK` notice goes out when free space is below 5%.
