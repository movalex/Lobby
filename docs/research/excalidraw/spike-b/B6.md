## F14 and the op budget

The room delays a push its op budget cannot pay (up to one burst waiting) and refuses with RATE beyond that
(O1-room.md deviation 1). The protocol twin and the spec push 12 × 500 creates at once from one session:
the first ~2,000 ops are applied, the next burst waits, the rest is RATE.
