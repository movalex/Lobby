### Corrections to the drill (found by its first runs through the browser)

Phase 1 wrote A's edits at the store level, where none of these could show:

1. **Locked shapes.** The tour has locked shapes, and the room refuses A's edits of them (`LOCKED`), as
   the product does. The drill no longer picks a locked shape for any role (A's or B's).
2. **The planted conflict's target.** A polygon geo (cloud, star, …: lossy `geo-polygon`) converts to a
   transparent container, an outline `line` (`<id>.o`) and a label. B's recolour lands on the outline's
   stroke, A's on the container's background: different elements, so no same-group conflict can be planted
   there. The target is now a note or a geo the converter maps to one native element (rectangle, ellipse,
   diamond).
3. **Nested frames and groups.** B's move of a frame moves every tldraw descendant (a nested frame and its
   note on the kitchen sink), not only the elements whose `frameId` is that frame; they are now counted as
   touched (not in "untouched byte-identical") and checked instead: every other descendant moved +120.
4. **Stricter:** "A created an element" now also requires it to be present (a lost create compared
   `undefined` with `undefined`), and every A edit is checked in the room's snapshot before R0.

## Findings

1. **The converted file is 2.3–4.6× the tldraw file** (heavy board 3.0 → 13.9 MB, `lb_elements` rows plus
   `lb_source`), above §5.3's estimate (`lb_*` 1.3–1.9× plus `lb_source` 1×). The disk check (3.5× + 50 MB)
   covers every board here only thanks to its margin; a 100 MB board would need ≈ 460 MB against a check of
   400 MB. Recommend 5× + 50 MB (§5.3 step 1) and re-checking Render's 1 GB disk budget.
2. **Arrow bindings:** `isExact` → mode `inside` at the anchor; otherwise `orbit` with the precise anchor or
   the centre (0.5001). The plan's "`isPrecise` → `inside`" would end precise arrows inside their targets.
3. **Arrow labels keep `labelPosition`:** the engine supports it (`getPointAtPathParameter`), so
   `arrow-label-position` no longer applies.
4. **Arrows are written with roughness 0** (C0 finding 2: rotated follows at roughness 1 can exceed the
   tolerance); tldraw's `draw` dash keeps roughness 1 on other shapes.
5. **Notes keep their tldraw height:** `baseHeight` = (200 + `growY`) × scale, so bounds hold; the engine
   fits the font within it.
6. **`drAi` and `drPrivateFrame` cannot be carried:** the registry's `ai {by}` and `privateFrame {session,
   by}` need an author the tldraw meta never recorded; reported as `ai-marker` / `private-frame-marker`.
   The template markers `draftroomPrompt`/`Slot`/`Swatch` needed registry keys (change request below).
7. **The roll-forward's base schema:** `converted_from` stores only `schemaHash`; the merge finds the base's
   schema text by that hash among `metadata.schema` and `dr_deleted_schemas`. If R0 migrated the schema and
   no row matches, the board stays closed (`base-schema-unknown`). Recommend storing the schema text with
   `lb_source` (a `room/schema.ts` change for wave 2D; O1 and O4).
8. **New lossy codes:** `group-with-frame`, `link-dropped`, `embed-as-link`, `bookmark-as-link`,
   `meta-dropped`, `ai-marker`, `private-frame-marker`, `empty-group`.
9. **(Phase 2) The converted boards satisfy the room's cross-element invariants.** O1's `checkInvariants`
   finds 0 violations on all 15 boards, on the converter's output and on the stored rows (labels one to one
   and after their containers, bindings to live bindable targets and symmetric, frames, group nesting,
   valid and unique indices), 3/3. So the room's repairs should have nothing to do on a converted board's
   first open (not run yet: conversion on open is not integrated).
10. **(Phase 2) The runtime cap's rule of thumb is at its edge.** The heavy board's peak total heap reached
    67.1 MB (phase 1: 65.3 MB); 1.5 × that is 100.7 MB against the 98 MB cap. The cap limits the old
    generation, and the board converts with 40 MB of it, so nothing fails; wave 2D should derive the cap from
    the measured old-generation floor over several runs.

11. **(Phase 3) The roll-forward could give a placed element a tombstone's z-order key** (fixed, `3390b0c`).
    Step f placed R0's new or re-ordered shapes between the LIVE keys around their paint-order neighbour; on
    the kitchen sink, R0 deleted a shape and added one right above that neighbour, and the new shape got the
    deleted one's key. Clients hold tombstones in their scene array, so Excalidraw's `syncInvalidIndices`
    re-keyed the tombstone on open and the tab pushed it back (an echo on every open of a rolled-forward
    board; the roll-forward's own index check counted live keys only and did not see it). Step f now keeps
    every key in use, tombstones included, and step g refuses a merge that wrote a key another element holds
    (`INDEX_DUPLICATE`). The new unit test fails before the fix (`'aE'` twice). Wave 2D: the same rule
    belongs in the roll-forward's property test, and O1 may want its invariant checker to count tombstones.
12. **(Phase 3) Conversion on open and the roll-forward on open work end to end.** Every board converted
    by the room's open path in the capped worker (the heavy board in 1.2–1.3 s, 62.7–64.4 MB total heap),
    both tabs converged with the snapshot and pushed nothing; after each roll-forward A's tab converged and
    pushed nothing, and the room's snapshot hash equalled the roll-forward report's `mergedHash` in every
    cycle. Both triggers of the roll-forward work: an internal route (the test snapshot) and a joiner held
    with `PREPARING` (O2's page shows no "Preparing this board…" state yet; the tab waited for its welcome).
13. **(Phase 3) The harness's `openBoard` can resolve before a reset welcome's snapshot is applied** (for
    O3). `waitBoardReady` waits for the facade's `session().welcomed`, which is the `welcome` message;
    the reset snapshot is the next frame. On the heavy board the tab's clock was 0 at `welcomed` and 1 a
    moment later (evidence run 1, cycle 1), so a `get` right after `openBoard` can return null. The drill
    waits for a quiet clock (1.5 s) before A edits; specs that act right after `openBoard` on a large board
    are exposed. It is also the likely cause of the one smoke-run failure on the heavy board (A's move not
    made).
14. **(Phase 3) Windows path length and the stand-in's backups.** With the stack's data dir deep in the
    scratchpad (the launcher's `dr-e2e-*` level included), today's backup pass failed with "unable to
    open database file" for every backup (the tmp file and its journal passed 260 characters), visible only
    in the log. The staging specs keep their paths short (`w3/<run>/d`) and assert the deepest backup path
    is under 250 characters. Not a Render issue (Linux); worth a log line that names the cause on Windows
    dev machines.
15. **(Phase 3) The store-level drill (`--excalidraw store`, phase 1's stand-in for A) is superseded**: its
    delete marks a container deleted without its label (the room cascades), so on the kitchen sink its
    roll-forward refuses the merge (`LABEL_ORPHANED`, `validation`). Phase 1's three boards were not
    affected; the evidence now runs A through the browser.
