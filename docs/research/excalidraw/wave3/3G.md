## 2. Deviations from today (and why)

1. **A card's face is a DOM overlay** over its `image` element, drawn in scene units and scaled with the
   zoom. Opaque elements painted above a card (notes, images and other cards, shapes with a solid fill)
   cut a hole in the face so they stay visible; frames never do (their children are drawn over them),
   and texts, strokes and unfilled shapes over a card are drawn under its face. The overlay is above the
   canvas, so the card's selection outline shows only outside its bounds. The canvas face (the worker's
   cover or poster, through the loader, stretched to the element) is what exports and thumbnails see.
2. **No hint props.** The tldraw card stored status, page count, duration and poster id as hints the
   uploading tab kept current. The element now holds only the stable facts (plan §2.10.4); until the
   api's meta answers, a card says "Loading" (`data-status="loading"`).
3. **Images appear once uploaded.** tldraw showed the uploading tab a local preview while uploading. The
   element is created only after the upload has returned the version id (§2.10.1), so a drop shows an
   "Uploading …" notice first (one for the drop's images, new copy: "Uploading N images…"), and then
   everyone sees the "Processing…" tile until the derivative exists (F4: no local preview).
4. **One undo step for a drop's images, created together** once all of their uploads have settled; a
   card lands as its own upload finishes (after the drop's images). Before, images landed at once.
5. **New elements are painted on top.** tldraw stacked an image dropped on a template slot "just above
   the slot"; `LobbyTx` has no insert-at-index (only front/back/forward/backward), so a slotted image or
   card goes on top of the frame's content. It stays inside the slot and covers only the slot.
6. **A centre crop is Excalidraw's `crop` in the thumb's pixels** (Excalidraw crops in the pixels of the
   file it has loaded), so a cropped image never switches to `display`.
7. **Animated GIFs (FIL-08) show their first frame** at every zoom: the plan defers inline playback
   (§4.1); the original is never loaded on the canvas.
8. **Double-click on any card opens the original in a new tab**, as today (the plan also allows the
   viewer or a player overlay; today's behaviour and its spec were kept).
9. **SVG, TIFF, HEIC** stay attachment cards (the api's `classifyUpload` calls only PNG, JPEG, WebP and
   GIF images), as today.
10. **The screen-reader descriptor** ("Word document: …", tldraw's shape-util aria descriptor) is the card
    face's `aria-description`; keyboard traversal of elements is 3i's.
11. **No `open-card` pointer tool:** the double-click comes through the canvas `input` stream for every
    role, which the tool was meant to provide for read-only roles.
12. **The selection bar's "Download original" and the context-menu entry** are registered; their shells
    are 3a's, so neither is visible or tested until 3a lands.
