## Not fixed here: CR-3C-W5-1 to 2C (W5-25, the leader's side)

The follower fits the leader's presence `view`. `canvas/board/session.ts` sends it on a pointer move over the
canvas and (since W3-6) on a camera move, never when the canvas changes size. A dock panel the leader opens
after their last move narrows the leader's canvas while the camera stays, so every follower keeps the wider
view until the leader moves: 320 scene units off at zoom 0.5 with a 320 px panel. The review's "about 420 units
off" on the facilitator's run, with the leader's Voting panel open, fits the same cause (not re-measured on
that board). Probe on this branch: the leader's `viewport.get()` width went from
2,790 to 2,150 when the Comments panel opened, the follower's `presence.peer(sid).view` stayed at 2,790 until
the leader's pointer moved.

**Request:** re-send presence when the canvas resizes, after Excalidraw has the new size. In the board's
ResizeObserver callback `getAppState().width` is still the old one (Excalidraw's own observer applies it with a
`setState`, and the `resize` event carries the old size too); a `setTimeout(…, 0)` from that callback read the
new one, a `requestAnimationFrame` did not. Re-sending from `onChange` when `width`, `height`, `offsetLeft` or
`offsetTop` changed would also give `resize` its new size (CR-3A-10). Checked with the timer version applied
locally and not committed: the spec below then passed.

Spec: `e2e/excalidraw/people-follow.spec.ts` "W5-25: a gathered desktop follower sits on the leader's view
centre when the leader's dock panel is open", `test.fail` naming CR-3C-W5-1. Its mark comes off with the fix.

The review's other W5-25 half, a phone landing at 14 % zoom, was R2-04, fixed at wave 3b (W3-10;
`workshops-follow` R2-04 passes here). The phone follower's centre has the same leader-side cause.
