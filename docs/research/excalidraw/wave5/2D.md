## 1. What changed

| Finding | On the new canvas | On the tldraw page |
|---|---|---|

| D4 (polygons) | Does not apply: the templates use only rectangles, ellipses and diamonds (`LobbyGeo`), so no template needs a polygon. | — |

**The display face waits for the server.** The scene sets text in fontFamily 7 only when the server's own
element check accepts it: `DISPLAY_FACE_AVAILABLE` runs `checkElement` on a probe text. That needs 2A's
allowlist (§3). Until then the headings are Nunito, and the type samples' roles say "· sans", so no label ever
names a face the canvas is not showing. When 2A's change merges, the headings switch by themselves. Every
template is still accepted as authored, before and after.

**New in `@draftroom/templates/scene`:** `DISPLAY_FACE_AVAILABLE`, `DISPLAY_FONT_FAMILY_ID` (7),
`FONT_FACE_NAME`, `FONT_FAMILY.display`, and `sceneFontLoads()`, the `document.fonts.load()` specs a browser
loads before it builds a scene.

**In the plan builder:** `TextSpec.heading`, `GeoSpec.heading`, `GeoSpec.scene.text` (a label's own words on
the new canvas), `TextSpec.scene.displayText` (the words once the display face is available), and the font
name `'display'` (tldraw sets it in sans).

## 2. Evidence

These were run in this worktree on Windows 11 with Node 24 and Playwright's headless Chromium. Ports
6920–6959 were used (`E2E_PORT_BASE=6920`), on a machine shared with other agents' runs. The gate itself is
in the workflow result and in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

- **Before the fix:**
  - `reading-order.test.ts` failed all 14 of its tests: every template, on both builds, read the intro first
    in at least one frame.
  - The page spec's W5-59 test failed on the tour's "1 · Welcome".
  - The W5-05 test, run without its mark, fails on the brainstorm. The page shows "Change not saved" and
    leaves the heading behind (the review's repro).
- **The display path:** `display-face.test.ts` simulates 2A's allowlist (canvas-schema's `checkElement`
  accepting 7 as it accepts 6). With it, every template comes out in Lilita One and Nunito, with the type
  samples' roles named. Everything stays inside its frame with no overlaps, measured with the Node table
  (which measures Lilita One as Nunito until 2A adds its metrics).
- **A look at the display face:** screenshots on the engine page with fontFamily 7 applied (Lilita One
  loaded) were checked by eye: the tour's Welcome, Brainstorm, Mood board and Tips frames, the retrospective,
  the brainstorm and the mood board. They were not committed.
- **Where the pillars stand:** the tour's three Welcome pillars ("Kick off", "Make", "Review": a title over a
  sentence) keep their label in Nunito. One label holds one face, and a whole sentence in the display face
  reads heavy. The title stays their plain first line. The review did not list them.
